✦ Calligrapher & Artist

✦ Hello there

I write with
ink & heart.

I'm a calligrapher and hand-lettering artist with a deep love for the meditative practice of writing beautifully. My journey began with a single dip pen and a piece of cartridge paper, and it has grown into a full studio practice.

Every piece I create carries a piece of me — the stillness of an early morning, the warmth of intention, the joy of giving words a form they deserve.

I work across styles from traditional Copperplate and Spencerian scripts to contemporary brush lettering and modern calligraphy — blending classic discipline with fresh expression.
