✦ Hello there

I write with
ink & heart.

I’m Oshin Saini, a self taught calligraphy artist and the creator behind Divine Ink. Over the years, I’ve had the opportunity to collaborate with poets from more than 35 countries and work with brands across over 10 countries, which has allowed my art to travel far beyond where it began.

For me, calligraphy is more than beautiful lettering. It is a way of turning emotions, thoughts, and stories into something meaningful. I love creating pieces that feel personal and carry a sense of warmth and connection.

Inspired by flowers, books, nature, and the beauty in quiet moments, I create art with intention, hoping each piece leaves a lasting feeling long after it is seen.

I work across styles from traditional Copperplate and Spencerian scripts to contemporary brush lettering and modern calligraphy — blending classic discipline with fresh expression.
